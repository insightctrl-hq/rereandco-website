    Our Story

    Crafted with Heart,
    Baked to Perfection.

    Born in the heart of Port Harcourt, Rereandco is a space where good food and great vibes naturally connect. What started as a passion for authentic baking has grown into a premier destination for memorable mornings and grand weekend brunches.

    Founded by Mrs. Seun Ekorie, Rereandco merges the precision of professional branding with the profound warmth of true hospitality. We understand that a meal is more than just sustenance; it is a visual and sensory experience.

    Using only the finest natural ingredients and time-honored techniques, we handcraft every plate and pastry daily. Whether you are grabbing a quick morning coffee or settling in for a luxurious weekend brunch, our mission remains the same: to ensure every bite is a moment of pure joy.
